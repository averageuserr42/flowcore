# Position on use

FlowCore exists for people who are having a hard time: it records where attention goes, where the body says stop, and what happened after. That is the only reason this file exists.

## Read this first

**This is a statement of the author's position, not a license condition.**

The code is released under the MIT license ([LICENSE](LICENSE)) without restrictions. MIT grants permission to any person who obtains a copy, and nothing on this page narrows that grant, adds a condition to it, or creates a legal restriction on use. A copy that ignores this file is still a lawful copy. The reason is plain: a public single-file app cannot check who downloaded it, so a clause pretending to control that would be theatre dressed as law.

## What this work is not for

The author does not want this software used by, or on behalf of:

- **government bodies** — any state, regional or local government agency, ministry or administrative department;
- **armed forces, intelligence agencies and law enforcement organisations**;
- **state-owned or state-controlled enterprises** — any company where a government holds a controlling stake or majority ownership;
- **political organisations** — parties, political action committees, campaign and lobbying entities;
- **defence contractors** — any private entity using this software directly to fulfil military, defence or state-security contracts.

**This is not aimed at people by passport.** Individuals anywhere — including countries whose governments are named above — are welcome here without qualification. The line is drawn at institutions and at the work they commission, not at nationality, citizenship or place of residence.

## What the author can actually do

- **A hosted version with accounts**, if one ever exists, will carry an enforceable version of this position in its terms of service: access can be revoked without notice or refund. That is a real mechanism, because a service controls its own doors. The code stays MIT either way.
- **No money from these entities.** The author will not accept sponsorship, commissions, consulting or investment from them.
- **Where support goes.** Money and effort the author directs around this project go to people harmed by the wars this position is about, and not to state structures.

## What the author cannot do

Prevent a copy. Anyone can take `flowcore.html`, rename it and publish it. The mitigation for that is not a license paragraph but reputation, a maintained original, and — if it ever matters — a service with an identity behind it. Pretending otherwise would be a lie told to feel better.

If you represent an organisation listed above: you are asked not to use this work. If you are a person using it to get through a bad week: this page is not about you.
